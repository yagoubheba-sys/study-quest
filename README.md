# ⚔️ Study Quest - Gamified Study Timer

A beautiful, interactive gamified study website with timer, XP system, leveling, and daily streaks!

## Features ✨

### 🎮 Core Gameplay
- **Study Timer**: 2-hour study sessions with 30-minute break timers
- **Start Quest Button**: Begins your study session with automatic timer
- **Pause & Reset**: Full control over your study sessions

### 📊 Progression System
- **XP System**: Earn 100 XP per completed study session
- **Leveling**: Level up as you accumulate XP (XP requirements increase by 10% per level)
- **Level Progress Bar**: Visual representation of progress to next level
- **Real-time Updates**: See your stats update instantly

### 🔥 Streak System
- **Daily Streak Counter**: Tracks consecutive days of studying
- **Auto Check-in**: Automatically checks you in when you complete a study session
- **Manual Check-in**: Can check in daily even without completing a session
- **Best Streak**: Tracks your highest streak achieved
- **Streak Persistence**: Your streak carries over across browser sessions

### 🏆 Achievements
Seven achievements to unlock:
- 👣 **First Step**: Complete your first study session
- 📚 **Studier**: Complete 5 sessions
- 🏃 **Marathon**: Complete 10 sessions
- 🔥 **7-Day Streak**: Maintain a 7-day streak
- 🌟 **30-Day Streak**: Maintain a 30-day streak
- ⭐ **Level 5**: Reach level 5
- 👑 **Level 10**: Reach level 10

### 📈 Statistics Dashboard
- **Sessions Completed**: Total number of study sessions finished
- **Total Study Time**: Cumulative hours and minutes studied
- **Current Level & XP**: Real-time progression display
- **Today's Status**: Shows if you've studied today with visual feedback

### 💾 Data Persistence
- All data saved to browser's localStorage
- Your stats, level, streak, and achievements persist between sessions
- No account login required

## How to Use 🚀

1. **Start Your Quest**: Click "▶ Start Quest" to begin a 2-hour study session
2. **Study**: Timer counts down. Use "⏸ Pause" if needed
3. **Complete Session**: When timer hits zero:
   - Earn 100 XP
   - Gain daily check-in (increases streak)
   - Get celebratory notification
4. **Take a Break**: Timer switches to 30-minute break automatically
5. **Track Progress**: Watch your level, XP, and achievements
6. **Maintain Streak**: Study every day to build your streak!

## Timer Structure 🕐

- **Study Phase**: 2 hours of focused studying
- **Break Phase**: 30 minutes to rest
- **Repeat**: Continue as many cycles as you want

## Tips for Success 💡

- Set consistent daily goals
- Track your progress with statistics
- Work towards specific achievements
- Higher levels require more XP - keep studying!
- Use "Check In Today" button to maintain streak on lighter days
